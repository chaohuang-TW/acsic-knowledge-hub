# ACSIC Knowledge Hub experience rebuild

## Scope and direction

Audience: international credit guarantee institution visitors and public-data researchers. The visitor should be able to find an institution immediately, or explore its geographic context without learning a new interface.

Direction: Living Atlas, a working atlas rather than a promotional landing page. Celadon and pear-white remain the brand anchors; dark green provides readable operational contrast. System sans-serif typography, structured rules, open layouts and restrained surfaces replace the oversized serif hero and repeated rounded containers. Motion is focused on selection and whole-mascot movement, never a prerequisite for content.

Design dials: visual variance 6, motion 3, density 4. Dense data tables and form controls prioritize clarity over landing-page styling.

## Browser-visible concepts

1. Living Atlas: compact editorial introduction and direct lookup alongside the geographic workspace. The two visitor paths are visible together.
2. Research Index: dark editorial masthead with a broad search surface, then a shallow full-width map and evidence index.
3. Network Notebook: numbered research journey with a narrow content column and map sheet, adapted to a sequential mobile journey.

These are real responsive HTML prototypes with working lookup/profile links and economy interaction, not image mockups. Local capture artifacts record desktop and mobile versions. The concepts use existing governed institution labels; no research content is invented.

## Selection criteria

Prefer the concept with the clearest first-screen lookup, recognizable geography, legible bilingual content, and lowest interaction cost. Preserve existing deep links, data meaning and research controls. No visual direction may weaken source traceability or make missing values resemble zero.

## Protected boundaries

Only this public repository is in scope. The private strategy repository, main homepage, redirect repository, custom domains and account settings are untouched. Research files and the original Meng-Ge WebP are checksum-protected. Map geometry is presentation data with separate provenance, not a research source.

## Acceptance discipline

Production-build browser checks, behavioral tests, accessibility and performance evidence precede release. Two final tours must have no new P0 or P1 issues. A screenshot demonstrates the inspected viewport only. Lab measurements do not establish field INP or physical-device behavior. If release gates remain incomplete, the work remains a draft and the production site is not updated.
