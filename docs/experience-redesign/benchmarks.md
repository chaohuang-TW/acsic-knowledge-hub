# Award-caliber research benchmarks

This note records the external references used for the Data, Compare and Reports
surfaces in the ACSIC Knowledge Hub experience rebuild. It is a design reference,
not a claim that ACSIC or the Knowledge Hub has received any of these awards.

## Selection and verification method

- Award identity, year, category, entrant and official work URL were checked on
  the award body's own record.
- Each current work URL below was opened in an isolated Chrome tab at the normal
  desktop viewport and again at an explicit 390 x 844 CSS-pixel viewport. At
  least one visible interaction was performed across the set; the result is
  recorded rather than inferred from an award thumbnail or search snippet.
- The current interface is recorded separately from the historical award record.
  A live URL is not assumed to be the same version that won.
- No repository files, third-party accounts or other agent tabs were changed.

## Verified reference set

### NASA Solar System Exploration — Webby Winner, 2022

- Award record: [The Webby Awards](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2022/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-websites-and-mobile-sites/science/212090/nasa-solar-system-exploration)
- Official work: `https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/`
- Case study: [Stories Behind the Sites](https://www.webbyawards.com/crafted-with-code/nasa-solar-system-exploration/)
- The official case study describes an “entity-focused” interactive visual
  encyclopedia and a “living site” that evolves with planetary missions.
- Desktop check: the current redirect exposed a clear hierarchy of Solar System
  Home, Planets, Moons, Asteroids & Comets, Skywatching, facts, mission links and
  the embedded Eyes on the Solar System experience.
- Mobile check (390 x 844): the current `science.nasa.gov/solar-system/` page
  collapsed the header to menu, NASA mark and search controls; the section
  navigation became an `Explore This Section` control while the hero and numeric
  facts remained legible.
- Interaction check: activating `Explore This Section` expanded the section
  navigation and exposed Planets, Moons, Asteroids & Comets, Skywatching and More.
- Current versus awarded: the old work URL now resolves into the current NASA
  Science structure and the page reported a 30 September 2026 update. This is
  current-site evidence only, not proof that the present version is the 2022
  award snapshot.

**Borrow:** entity-first navigation, latest/current content cues and a path from
overview to evidence.

**Do not copy:** NASA's scale, real-time space simulation or mission-specific
visual language. ACSIC must not imply real-time or complete global coverage.

### The 1619 Project — Webby Winner and People's Voice Winner, 2022

- Award record: [The Webby Awards](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2022/websites-and-mobile-sites/general-desktop-mobile-sites/education/211989/the-1619-project)
- Official work: `https://1619education.org/`
- The official record lists two 2022 wins: Webby Winner and People's Voice
  Winner, Websites and Mobile Sites / Education.
- Desktop check: the current page exposed distinct routes for the original issue,
  curricular resources, Defining Democracy, updates, student work and community.
- Mobile check (390 x 844): the page became a readable single-column flow with a
  compact header, primary-menu toggle, original-issue links, resource collections,
  dates and latest updates.
- Interaction check: activating the mobile primary-menu toggle expanded the
  visible navigation, including Curricular Resources, Defining Democracy,
  Program News and Related Reporting.
- Current versus awarded: the live page now includes 2026 resource and update
  entries. It is a maintained current publication, not a replay of the 2022
  award-era content.

**Borrow:** keep original source, resource collections, updates and community
paths distinct; show dates and source relationships; provide audience-specific
routes before asking visitors to interpret data.

**Do not copy:** the project's historical/political subject matter, editorial
volume or narrative framing. ACSIC remains an independent, unofficial platform.

### The Pudding — Webby Honoree, 2019

- Award record: [The Webby Awards](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2019/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-data-visualization/86577/the-pudding)
- Official work: `https://pudding.cool/`
- The award record says **Honoree**, not Winner, in 2019 for Best Data
  Visualization.
- Desktop check: the current home page exposed a story-search field and filters
  for Our Faves, Popular, Updating, Your Input, Video and Audio.
- Mobile check (390 x 844): the site reduced the header to the Pudding mark and
  menu button, retained story search and a native filter control, and switched
  story cards to a single-column reading flow.
- Interaction check: selecting `Our Faves` changed the selected option and the
  visible story set (for example, to the site's older favourite projects),
  confirming a real filter transition rather than a decorative control.
- Current versus awarded: the current home page is a living publication with
  2026 stories and later data work; current stories are not retroactively treated
  as the 2019 award work.

**Borrow:** searchable story/evidence cards, concise summaries, tags and dates,
and a progressive path from narrative overview to dataset detail.

**Do not copy:** newsroom scale, playful illustration style or unsupported
interpretive storytelling. ACSIC's period, unit, source and definition remain
visible next to every number.

### Siena Film Foundation — Awwwards Site of the Day, 2025

- Award record: [Awwwards — Siena Film Foundation](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/siena-film-foundation)
- Official work: `https://siena.film/`
- The official record identifies **Site of the Day**, 18 March 2025, with a
  7.9/10 SOTD score and a separate Developer Award score. Its evaluation system
  exposes the public weighting of Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20% and
  Content 10%.
- Desktop check: the current site opened with a black Siena Film Foundation
  entry screen and an `ENTER` control; after activation it exposed film cards,
  left-side film navigation, right-side navigation and `SEE CASE STUDY` /
  `EXPLORE` routes.
- Mobile check (390 x 844): the current site changed to a vertical film-card
  layout with a compact menu button, readable metadata and an `EXPLORE` action.
- Interaction check: activating `ENTER` moved from the entry screen to the film
  catalogue, exposing the film navigation and case-study routes.
- Current versus awarded: the current catalogue includes 2025 and 2026 film
  entries and a current copyright line. The award page establishes the 2025
  recognition, but the current site is not treated as an archived award snapshot.

**Borrow:** a clearly staged entry into a dense catalogue, strong section labels,
and direct paths from an overview card to a detailed case study.

**Do not copy:** immersive film presentation, infinite-scroll pacing or dark
cinematic art direction where they would hide ACSIC's units, sources or period
labels.

### HeronAI — FWA of the Day, 2026

- Award record: [The FWA — HeronAI](https://thefwa.com/cases/heronai)
- Official work: the FWA record's `Launch Project` link points to
  `https://heronaiapp.com/`.
- The official case page identifies **FWA of the Day**, 1 October 2026, and
  records 83 points at the time of inspection. It describes the experience as an
  interactive agent demonstration for architects.
- Desktop check: the live page used a measured architectural sketch, a persistent
  grid/coordinate language, explicit product navigation and a clear transition
  from demonstration to explanatory sections.
- Mobile check (390 x 844): the same URL switched to a narrow vertical layout,
  retained the menu, core hero statement, descriptive text, image sequence and
  accessible carousel controls.
- Interaction check: dragging across the architectural sketch changed the visible
  coordinate readout and revealed red violation markers / code callouts, matching
  the behavior described on the official FWA case page. No form was submitted.
- Current versus awarded: the official case and the current launch URL point to
  the same live project and both identify 2026, but no archival screenshot proves
  pixel identity with the award-time build. This is therefore recorded as a
  current live-site check, not a historical reconstruction.

**Borrow:** make the primary interaction explain the product or research model,
show state changes immediately, and keep a non-interactive explanatory path
alongside the demonstration.

**Do not copy:** commercial AI claims, building-code content, cursor-driven
effects or a demo that requires specialised domain knowledge. ACSIC's controls
must remain transparent, keyboard-usable and evidence-led.

## Official evaluation lenses

- The [official Webby criteria](https://www.webbyawards.com/judging-criteria/)
  provide the clearest public checklist: useful content, understandable
  structure, working functionality, meaningful interactivity, responsible
  innovation and overall experience.
- The [Awwwards evaluation system](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/)
  publishes a design/usability/creativity/content weighting of 40/30/20/10 for
  the SOTD score. It is a design-review lens, not a measure of ACSIC data
  quality.
- FWA's [official rules](https://thefwa.com/rules) and [25-year statement](https://thefwa.com/FWA25/25.html)
  frame recognition around original digital creativity, technical execution and
  innovation; no public numeric rubric was used here.

The implementation therefore keeps raw values, readable values, unit, currency,
period, source and export controls together; uses local comparison tables with a
mobile overflow boundary; and keeps report preview/export controls visible and
deterministic.

## Superseded references

The active set intentionally replaces older or unverifiable items from the first
draft: the 2012 Velvet Foundation URL is currently contaminated with unrelated
SEO content, the 2015 Ayaka microsite could not be reliably reopened, and the
Smithsonian Learning Lab check stopped at a verification gate. Their official
award records were not used to make current-interface claims.

## Limitations

- Award pages establish award identity, not the accuracy of ACSIC data.
- The mobile checks above are browser viewport checks at 390 x 844, not physical
  device, network-throttling, touch-latency or screen-reader acceptance tests.
- Current sites can change after this note; the observed date is 1 October 2026.
- HeronAI is an adjacent commercial product benchmark, not a content or policy
  model for ACSIC.
- No external translation API, tracking code, login or third-party data source
  was used.
