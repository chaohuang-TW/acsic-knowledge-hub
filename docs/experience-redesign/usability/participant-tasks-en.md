# First visit to ACSIC Knowledge Hub

These tasks help us understand the website, not assess your ability. Please tell us what you are thinking as you go. There is no required navigation path. You may pause, skip a task or stop. Please avoid sharing personal or confidential information.

## A. An institution's official website

Find Korea's KODIT and open its official website.

## B. Taiwan institutions

Find the Taiwan institutions included on this website. Are their ACSIC membership statuses the same? Explain what you found.

## C. Understanding two financial amounts

Find the scale of loan financing supported by ACGF in 2025. Find another number that represents a guarantee amount. Explain how the two amounts differ and find the official evidence for them.

## D. Japan institutions and returning

Find the two Japan institutions. Read the full profile of either one, then return to the context in which you were looking for it.

## E. Comparison

Compare two institutions. Add one more institution, then remove one. Explain which institutions are now being compared.

## F. Mobile exploration

On a mobile phone, explore three countries/economies consecutively. Find the profile of one institution in one of them.

## After the tasks

What was most confusing? What would make your next visit easier? Was anything difficult to read or operate?
