# Facilitator guide / 主持人指南

**Not yet executed / 尚未執行。** This guide contains a protocol, not participant findings. 此文件是執行流程，不是真人測試結果。

## Before the session / 開始前

1. Obtain participation consent, explain that the website—not the person—is being evaluated, and offer breaks or stopping. 不測驗受測者能力；說明可休息或停止，取得參與同意。
2. Ask which interface language and broad device category they will use. Assign an anonymous ID such as P01. Only confirm verbally that this is their first visit; do not collect identity information. 詢問介面語言與裝置類型，指定匿名代號；口頭確認首次使用，不收集身分資訊。
3. Start at the public homepage in that language. Do not demonstrate controls. Record the actual release SHA or dated build being tested. 從該語言的公開首頁開始，不示範操作；記錄實際受測版本。
4. Recording is optional and requires separate explicit consent. Do not upload private audio/video, screen contents from other apps, or participant personal information. 錄影另需明確同意；不要上傳私密影音、其他應用程式內容或個資。

## Neutral introduction / 中立開場

“We are evaluating how this website works for a first-time visitor. There are no right or wrong ways to navigate. Please tell us what you are looking for and what you expect as you go. You can skip a task or stop at any time.”

「我們想了解第一次來到本站的人如何使用它；不是測驗你。請一邊操作，一邊說出你在找什麼、期待看到什麼。可以跳過題目或隨時停止。」

## Running tasks / 進行任務

- Give tasks A–F one at a time, using the selected language sheet. Do not reveal buttons, control positions, URLs or a planned click path. 依序提供 A–F，不透露按鈕、位置、網址或預定點選路徑。
- Start timing when the task is given; end when the participant states completion, stops, or is interrupted. Record interruption separately. 發題開始計時，完成、停止或中斷時結束；中斷須另註。
- Ask neutral prompts: “What are you expecting?” / “What does this information mean to you?” Avoid “Try the search box” or “Click the country selector.” 可問「你預期會看到什麼？」及「這項資訊對你代表什麼？」；不要提示操作元件。
- If the person is stuck, ask whether they want a hint. Record the exact hint and mark the outcome as assisted. Do not silently count a prompted result as independent success. 卡住時先問是否需要提示，記錄提示原文；不要把受提示後完成算成獨立成功。
- Definitions: `success` = objective met without procedural help; `partial` = part met or procedural help needed; `failure` = objective not met; `not_attempted` = task not run. Also record hints independently so the distinction is visible. 結果定義：獨立達成、部分／協助達成、未達成、未執行；提示另行紀錄。
- Count misclicks as unintended activations reported by the participant or clearly followed by correction; count backtracks as returns caused by a wrong path. Do not classify exploratory reading or normal return navigation as an error. 不把探索性閱讀或正常返回當誤點／回頭錯誤。

## Recording and closing / 記錄與結束

Use `results-template.csv`. Keep unexecuted cells empty. Quote actual words only; avoid identifying details and ask before retaining quotations. If a quotation would identify someone, omit or anonymize it with their agreement. Never create fictional participant quotes.

使用空白 CSV，未執行欄位留白。原話只記實際說法，保留前先確認，排除身分線索；不得捏造引言。

Ask: “What was most confusing?” / “What would make the next visit easier?” / “Was anything hard to read or operate?” Thank the participant and stop recording. Summarize actionable observations privately before deciding what anonymized aggregate information may be published; public publication is not authorized by this kit alone.

結束時問「最困惑的是哪裡？」「下次怎樣會更容易？」「有沒有難讀或難操作的地方？」。先私下整理，再另行決定是否公開匿名摘要；此套件本身不授權公開受測結果。
