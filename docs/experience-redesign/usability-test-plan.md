# First-use usability test kit / 首次使用者測試套件

Status: **prepared; real participants not yet tested**. 狀態：**套件已備妥；真人使用者驗收待執行**。

This is an exploratory study for 3–5 people who have not previously used ACSIC Knowledge Hub. It is not statistically representative, a certification, an external award assessment, or evidence that physical iPhone Safari has been tested. Automated E2E, developer walkthroughs and AI-agent reviews are separate engineering evidence and are not participant sessions.

本套件供約 3–5 位從未使用本站的人進行探索性測試；不代表統計母體、無障礙認證或外部獎項驗收。E2E、開發者巡覽及代理審查不列為真人測試。實體 iPhone 與原生文字縮放仍須另行實測。

## Participants and permission / 對象與授權

The project owner may arrange 3–5 first-time visitors, ideally covering an unfamiliar business colleague, a general visitor, a mobile visitor, an English-interface visitor, and someone who benefits from larger text or easier controls. These characteristics may overlap in one person; do not collect disability or employment details.

由專案負責人另行安排約 3–5 位首次使用者；建議涵蓋不熟悉本站的業務同仁、一般訪客、手機使用者、英文介面使用者及需要較大文字或容易操作控制的人。同一位可涵蓋多種情境，不需記錄身分、任職單位或醫療資訊。

No invitations have been sent, contacts accessed, recruitment purchased, or personal information uploaded by this work. Participation and any optional recording require separate consent. Recording is not required. Private recordings must never be added to the public repository or evidence artifact.

本輪未寄送邀請、存取聯絡人、付費招募或上傳個資。參與及選擇性錄影均須另行取得同意；不需要錄影也能執行測試。私人錄影不得加入公開 repository 或 artifact。

## Session / 執行方式

Allow approximately 25–35 minutes, with breaks or early stopping at the participant's request. Show only the participant task sheet. Ask the participant to think aloud without teaching navigation, naming controls, or pointing at buttons. For task F, use a real mobile device if available and record its category honestly; desktop emulation is not a physical phone test.

每場約 25–35 分鐘，可休息或隨時停止。只提供受測任務表，不預教介面；鼓勵描述想法，主持人不得提示按鈕位置。任務 F 如使用實體手機，記錄實際裝置類型；模擬器不得寫成實體手機。

Use anonymous IDs such as P01. Do not record names, email addresses, employer names, account IDs, or exact device identifiers. Record task outcome, start/end timestamps, misclicks, backtracks, hints, confusion, comprehension, optional verbatim comments and suggested improvements. Unrun rows remain blank; never infer success from silence or fill invented observations.

僅使用 P01 等匿名代號。不得記錄姓名、電子郵件、任職單位、帳號或裝置識別碼。記錄每題開始／結束時間、結果、誤點、回頭、提示、困惑、理解、原話及改善建議。未執行欄位保持空白，不得以沉默推定成功或填入虛構結果。

## Acceptance and follow-up / 驗收與後續

Engineering/design polish and the test kit can be released without claiming human acceptance. After actual sessions, summarize observed barriers separately; classify severity using impact, frequency and available alternatives. Preserve whether assistance was needed. Do not average scores from 3–5 sessions into a representative usability rating.

工程精修與套件可先交付，但真人驗收須維持「待執行」。實際測試後，另整理障礙、影響、頻率、替代操作及提示情形；不要把 3–5 場結果換算為具代表性的評分。
