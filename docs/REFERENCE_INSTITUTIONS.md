# Reference Institutions

The seven reference institutions are research anchors, not a performance ranking.

| Institution | Institutional role                       | Research use                                                |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| JFC         | Policy finance institution               | Complete Level 2 governance baseline                        |
| ACGF        | Agricultural credit guarantee fund       | Agriculture, fisheries and cooperative-finance case         |
| KOTEC       | Technology finance guarantee institution | Technology appraisal, guarantee, investment and transfer    |
| DCGF        | Deposit and credit guarantee fund        | Dual-mandate boundary case                                  |
| JFG         | Guarantee federation                     | Federation, coordination and shared-service case            |
| SLECIC      | Export credit insurer and guarantor      | Export insurance and bank-guarantee case                    |
| SMEC        | SME development agency                   | Boundary between development support and guarantee delivery |

Selection records, bilingual rationales and review dates are governed in `src/data/reference-set.ts`. Institution records expose the selection through `referenceSet`, `referenceSetRole`, `referenceSetRationale` and `referenceSetVerifiedDate`.

Complete status is never assumed from selection. Every institution continues to pass the same centralized role-aware Level 2 standard.
