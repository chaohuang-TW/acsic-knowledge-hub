# User-first UX Simplification

## Before

The primary navigation and first-read surfaces foregrounded research workflow concepts such as Level 2, confidence, readiness and evidence coverage.

## After

The primary journey is ACSIC, Institutions, Compare, Data and Resources. Research and methodology content remains available from the footer, resources, profile disclosures and data methodology disclosures.

## Navigation

The main navigation now has five user tasks. Existing deep routes remain unchanged and are available through research and methodology links.

## Hidden research metadata

Member cards, comparison selectors and first-read data cards no longer foreground completeness, confidence, priority or readiness metadata. These data remain intact and are available in collapsed research details, source pages and methodology views.

## Data-model boundary

No production institution, source registry, Level 2, confidence, field-evidence, Level 3, provenance or readiness data model was changed by this UX work.
